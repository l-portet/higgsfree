# Provider logo sources

The marks in `marks.ts` are rendered monochrome (`currentColor`) at 24×24 and are used only to
identify which company serves a model. **All logos are trademarks of their respective owners, used
nominatively to identify the model provider; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.** If you are
a rights holder and want a mark changed or removed, open an issue.

Normalisation applied to every mark: single path, `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`, larger dimension scaled to
24 units and centred, then an optical trim of 4–8 % on the heaviest solid marks so the set reads at
one weight at 14–20 px. Colors, gradients and wordmarks were dropped.
